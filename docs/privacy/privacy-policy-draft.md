## In short
Paisa Vault works on your phone. It has no server and no account. We do not collect, receive or sell your data.

## What the app reads
- **Bank and UPI SMS** received after you install the app. The app uses them only to record your transactions. It ignores OTP and promotional messages. It does not read older messages.
- **Card details you add**, such as the number and expiry. On Android you can tap your card (NFC). On iPhone you can scan it with the camera. The camera picture is not saved. The CVV is never stored. Saving a card PIN is optional and off by default.

## How it is used
An AI model that is part of the app runs on your phone to read each message. Nothing is sent to any AI service.

## Where it is kept
On your phone, in an encrypted database. Card numbers and hidden transactions are in a second encrypted vault that opens only with your PIN, fingerprint or Face ID. Phone cloud backups are switched off for this data.

## Sharing
- By default nothing is shared.
- If you pair with a family member, transactions you mark as shared are sent directly to their phone over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. Hidden transactions are never shared.
- Encrypted backup and export files go only where you choose to send them.

## How long we keep it
Raw SMS text is deleted after [30 or 90] days. Everything else stays until you delete it. **Erase everything** in Settings removes all data from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Your choices
You can deny SMS permission and add transactions by hand. You can delete any transaction, card or rule. You can turn off notifications.

## Security
Encryption keys are held in the phone's secure hardware. The app locks with PIN or biometrics. Screenshots are blocked on card screens.

## Children
The app is not meant for children under 18.
